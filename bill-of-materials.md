# Bill of materials

> [!WARNING]
> Be prepared: this list may not be correct/up to date and something may be missing.
> Note: materials for the microswitch leg tips are not included in this BOM.

**Servos**

- 18 pcs RD3235SG servo
- 18 pcs Metal servo horn

**Screws**

- 18 pcs M2 x 6 hex screw countersunk
- 18 pcs M2 x 10 hex screw countersunk
- 75 pcs M2.5 x 10 hex screw DIN912
- 10 pcs M3 x 10 hex screw DIN912

**Threaded inserts**

- 36 pcs M2x3x3.2 
- 75 pcs M2.5x6x3.5
- 35 pcs M3x6x5

**Electronics**

- 1 pcs Pimoroni Servo 2040
- 1 pcs Raspberry Pi 5, 2 GB minimum.  
- 1 pcs Single channel relay, 5 V
- 1 pcs 2.08 inch, 256x64 SH1122 oled display ([reference](images/oled-display-reference.jpg))
- 1 pcs 5v, 5A Ubec
- 1 pcs MTS-101 toggle switch
- 2 pcs 4 mm bullet connector (when using a 4mm connector battery)
- 1 pcs usb type-c male breakout/test board
- 1 m AWG16 wire red
- 1 m AWG16 wire black
- 1 m AWG20 wire red
- 1 m AWG20 wire black
- Gpio jumper wires, female-female, various colors
- 1x40pin 2.54mm male pin headers
- Heatshrink, various ID

**Battery and charger**

- 1 pcs ~6000 mAh 2S LiPo, hard case , 4 mm bullet connectors
- 1 pcs LiPo charger

**Misc**
- 6 pcs (as pictured) rubber vacuum caps, ~6mm ID
- 6 pcs (alternative) clothes hanger rubber end caps, 6mm ID
- 2 kg PLA or PETG filament
- 5 ml normal/blue loctite

**Tools(required)**

- Soldering iron
- Wire cutter
- Wire stripper
- 3D printer, build area > 24 cm
- M2, M2.5, M3 hex keys
- Phillips head screwdriver
